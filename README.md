# CampusOne - Student Academic Suite 🎓

**CampusOne** is an all-in-one offline-first academic management app for modern university students built with **Flutter**, **Dart**, and **Hive**.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Routine & Schedule**: Class schedules, room tracking, and day-by-day routines.
- 📚 **Academic Hub**: Subject tracking, course materials, credit hours, and GPA calculations.
- ⏱️ **Productivity Suite**: Pomodoro study timers, weekly study analytics charts, habit tracking, and goal settings.
- 📝 **Rich Notes Editor**: Rich-text study notes powered by Flutter Quill, categorized by folders.
- 📄 **Cover Page Generator**: Professional assignment cover page creator with instant PDF preview, export, and sharing.
- 💰 **Expense & Budget Manager**: Semester expense logger, category budgets, and financial statistics.
- 🏫 **Campus Directory**: Faculty contact list, office locations, and interactive campus map.
- 🔍 **Search Everywhere**: Global search across all notes, routines, subjects, faculty, and expenses.
- 🔐 **Security & App Lock**: Biometric (fingerprint/face) app lock protection.
- 💾 **Data Backup & Restore**: Zip-compressed `.campusone` data backup export & import.
- 🚀 **Onboarding & Splash**: Smooth animated splash screen and first-time student onboarding setup.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework**: Flutter 3.x (Dart SDK 3.12+)
- **State Management**: Provider
- **Local Database**: Hive (NoSQL, fast binary key-value storage)
- **PDF & Document Engine**: `pdf` & `printing`
- **Security**: `local_auth` (Biometric Fingerprint & Face Unlock)
- **Charts & UI**: `fl_chart`, `google_fonts`, `flutter_staggered_animations`
- **Notifications**: `flutter_local_notifications`
